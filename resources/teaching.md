# Teaching & Course Design
{: .no_toc }

Resources for instructors and teaching teams who want to incorporate computation and data science into their courses.

## Course Design

Resources for planning where and how computation or data science can fit into a course, including choosing tools, designing learning activities, and integrating computational work into existing coursework.

[Course Design Institute](https://teaching.utoronto.ca/cdi/) - U of T program focused on inclusive, accessible, and flexible course design.

[CTSI Workshops & Programs](https://teaching.utoronto.ca/workshops-programs/) - Workshops and programs on course design, teaching, and pedagogy.

## Assignment Design

Resources for designing programming and data-focused assignments, including how to structure tasks, support students through different stages of an assignment, and provide feedback.

[UDL Conversations: Assessment Design](https://teaching.utoronto.ca/teaching-uoft-udl/udl-programming/udl-conversations/assessment-design/) - Examples and strategies for designing flexible and meaningful assessments.

[Assessment & Automated Testing]({{ '/resources/assessment/' | relative_url }}) - CDSE resources for MarkUs, automated testing, and other assessment tools.

## Teaching with Computational Tools

Resources for using tools such as Jupyter, JupyterLab, and RStudio in teaching and coursework.

[JupyterHub Guide](https://teaching.utoronto.ca/tool-guides/jupyterhub/) - U of T guide to using Jupyter Notebook, JupyterLab, and RStudio through the University's educational JupyterHub.

[Python & Jupyter Resources]({{ '/resources/python/' | relative_url }})

[R & RStudio Resources]({{ '/resources/r/' | relative_url }})

## Accessibility & Inclusive Teaching

Resources for making computational course materials, activities, and technologies more accessible and inclusive.

[Teaching and Universal Design for Learning at U of T](https://teaching.utoronto.ca/teaching-uoft-udl/) - U of T resources and strategies for designing flexible and accessible learning experiences.

[Design for Inclusion](https://teaching.utoronto.ca/edtech/include/) - Guidance on accessible and inclusive use of educational technologies.

## Additional Teaching Resources

[Centre for Teaching Support & Innovation](https://teaching.utoronto.ca/) - University-wide teaching resources, consultations, workshops, and programs.

[CTSI Teaching Priorities](https://teaching.utoronto.ca/teaching-priorities/) - Guidance on areas including UDL, teaching with TAs, generative AI, and reflective teaching.
